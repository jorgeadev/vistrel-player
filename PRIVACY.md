# Vistrel Player privacy policy

_Last updated: November 22, 2026_

Vistrel Player is a video player that runs on your PC. It is built to keep what you watch private.

## What the app collects

**Nothing.** Vistrel Player has no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry and no ads. It does not send information about you, your files or what you watch to the developers or to anyone else.

## What stays on your PC

- **Log files**, used only to diagnose problems. They are stored in the app's private folder, kept for 7 days, and deleted when you uninstall the app. They never leave your PC unless you choose to attach them to a bug report.
- **Settings and playback history** (for example, where you stopped in a video), stored in the app's private folder.

## Network access

The current version does not connect to the internet unless you open an online stream yourself.

Later versions will offer optional features that use the network, such as downloading an AI subtitle model. These will be off until you turn them on, will only download files from the sources named in the app, and will check each download against a known checksum. This policy will be updated before any such feature ships.

## Contact

Report questions or concerns by opening an issue in the project's GitHub repository.
